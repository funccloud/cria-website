the problem

Parenting is already a full-time job.

Finding the right swim class, the perfect art workshop, or figuring out if there's still a spot in that soccer camp — it's exhausting. You search everywhere. Ask in Facebook groups. Text other parents. And still end up uncertain.

There has to be a better way.

Cria makes it simple.

1. discover

Tell us about your child (age, interests, neighborhood) and Cria shows you the best local activities, personalized just for them.

2. book

Reserve a spot in seconds. No back-and-forth, no waiting for a reply. Just tap and done.

3. manage

One calendar for the whole family. Schedules, reminders, and everything in one place — for every kid.

about cria

It takes a village.

Cria was built by parents, for parents. We believe every child deserves access to enriching experiences — and every parent deserves a little less chaos. We connect Miami families with trusted local teachers, coaches, and activity providers. All in one place, finally.

Our mission: make the village real.
